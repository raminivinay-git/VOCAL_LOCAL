# 🎙️ VOCAL LOCAL — Voice & Visual Hyperlocal Commerce App

**VOCAL LOCAL** is an accessible, picture-first, and Telugu voice-guided (`te-IN`) hyperlocal commerce web application designed for **Shopkeepers (Vendors)**, **Customers**, and **Delivery Partners**—built so that even elderly or non-literate users can easily buy, sell, and deliver local groceries, artisan handcrafts, and raw materials.

## ✨ Key Features

1. **Three Unified Role Interfaces (`🏪 Vendor`, `🛒 Customer`, `🛵 Delivery Partner`)**
   - **Vendor Self-Delivery Mode (`🏪➡️🛵`)**: Shopkeepers can toggle on Delivery Partner mode to deliver orders themselves and earn 100% of the delivery commission.
2. **Elder-Friendly Telugu Voice Guide (`🔊 తెలుగు వాయిస్`)**
   - Speaks item names, prices, cart summaries, wallet balances, chat messages, and 4-digit OTPs slowly and clearly in Telugu (`te-IN` at `0.84x` speed) as well as Hindi, Tamil, and English.
3. **One-Time Swiggy-Style Login**
   - Login with Email/Phone & Password or **Sign in with Google**; session persists automatically in `localStorage`.
4. **25+ Realistic 3D-Animated Product Families (Artisan Crafts, Handcraft Materials & Groceries)**
   - Includes Etikoppaka wooden lacquer toys, terracotta pots & diyas, Kalamkari handloom fabric, silk zari threads & beads kits, bamboo baskets, brass lamps, handmade jute bags, lac bangles, herbal soaps, forest honey, spices, fruits, bakery items, and farm staples.
5. **Shopkeeper (Vendor) Full Item Control**
   - Add items via Mother-Tongue Voice or Typing (with live 3D picture matching and interactive price confirmation), toggle **In Stock / Out of Stock**, adjust inventory quantities (`+5` / `-5`), edit exact prices (`✏️ Edit Price`), or delete items (`🗑️`).
6. **Customer Shopping, 10-Minute Add-Item Timer, Live GPS Map & GST Invoice**
   - Order by voice or search, confirm with giant Green **OK** / Red **NO** buttons, choose **Cash on Delivery (COD)** or **Online Payment**, add forgotten items within a **10:00 countdown timer**, track the driver on a **Live GPS Route Map (`🗺️`)**, and print/download an **Official GST Retail Bill / Invoice (`🧾`)**.
7. **Security Credentials, Two-Way Translated Chat & 4-Digit OTP Verification**
   - Displays Verified **Driving Licence (DL)**, **Aadhaar (UIDAI)**, and **PAN / Police Clearance** for delivery trust, translates chat messages into Telugu/Hindi/Tamil with `🔊 Listen`, and verifies a **4-Digit Customer OTP** at the doorstep.
8. **Separate Role Wallets, COD Payout Schedules & 30% Vendor Protection**
   - Separate wallets and downloadable End-of-Day CSV/TXT statements for all 3 roles, Daily/Weekly/Monthly COD payout options (with instant settlement for online payments), and automatic **30% vendor compensation** on doorstep cancellation or payment failure.

## 🚀 Quick Start & GitHub Pages Deployment

Open `index.html` directly in any browser, or enable **GitHub Pages** on your repository (`Settings -> Pages -> Deploy from branch: main / root`) to host it live immediately.
